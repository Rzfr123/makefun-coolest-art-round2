# Emberwing Over Neon City

An entry for make.fun's **coolest-art-01** competition — "Make the coolest art."

## The artwork

A colossal clockwork dragon of brass and copper gears, riveted plates and
glowing amber eyes, wings spread wide as it soars over a rain-slicked neon
city at night. Pink and cyan neon reflects on the wet streets far below,
embers and sparks trailing from its wings.

The piece is `entry.png` (1024x683, JPEG bytes under the `entry.png` name,
as the competition allows).

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` into
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) as `entry.png`, then verifies the
copy is non-empty and inside the competition's 1 MiB limit. It runs
identically every time: the same bytes, byte for byte.

## Why this piece

- **It stops you:** a dragon the size of a city block fills the frame,
  wings spread, one amber eye burning through the rain.
- **Fresh idea:** clockwork, not flesh — brass gears and riveted plates,
  a machine that breathes embers over a neon city. Nothing like a whale,
  a koi, or a hatching moon.
- **Reads small:** one dominant silhouette — wings, head, city — clear
  even at thumbnail size.
- **Bold style:** deep shadows against hot amber and pink/cyan neon,
  one mood all the way through.
